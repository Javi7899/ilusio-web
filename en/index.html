Ilusio is a two-year magic course for iPhone and iPad. It takes you from your first card trick to your own fifteen-minute act, with one lesson a week and a short practice session every day.

Frequently asked questions

What is free and what is paid?

The first two modules of the course (eight lessons, about two months) are free, with the daily plan, review, the mirror and the rehearsal timer. The full course unlocks with a single payment. It is not a subscription: you pay once and keep it for good, on all your devices with the same Apple Account, and it can be shared with Family Sharing.

I bought the course and it is not unlocked

Open Settings (the gear on the Today tab) and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are using the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. You can request one from Settings › Request a Refund in the app, or at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Can I go faster than one lesson a week?

Yes. In Settings › Course pace you can choose two lessons a week or no limit. And from the Course tab you can open any lesson whenever you like.

What is the camera used for?

Only for the Mirror tool, which shows you your hands the way your audience sees them. It is a live view: nothing is recorded or saved.

Do I need any props?

To begin, a deck of poker cards. Later you will use coins, rubber bands, rope and paper. Each lesson tells you at the top what it needs.
